Your UAE bank already texts you every transaction. Cashiro reads those SMS on the phone and turns them into a private expense tracker — FAB, ENBD, Liv, ADCB, Mashreq, Emirates Islamic — in English AED. 153 banks and payment services across 23 countries.

★ SMS permission
Reading bank SMS is how the app works. Parsing and storage stay on the device. No account, no messages sent. You can still add transactions by hand.

★ Firefly III (optional)
One-way PAT push. Cashiro creates and updates only journals it owns (external_id pennywise-{hash}). Firefly-native transactions are never imported or overwritten. Map local accounts and categories to Firefly dropdowns.

★ AI (bring your own key)
Paste an OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, or xAI key. No built-in model is required.

★ Private by design
No ads, no trackers, no Cashiro server. Open source (AGPL-3.0).

Based on PennyWise AI, with Cashiro visuals and Firefly sync.
